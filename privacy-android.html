Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Nine to Wonderland (package com.aiwaint.ninetowonderland, the “App”), published by Aiwa Int (“we”, “us”).

Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. We operate no accounts, no servers of our own, and we ask you for no personal information. Your game progress, settings, chosen nickname and language are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to us.

2. Data collected by third-party services

Advertising — Google AdMob. The App shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch, and full-screen interstitial ads between duels. Google AdMob may collect and process your Advertising ID (AD_ID), device and operating-system information, IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Online multiplayer — Ably Realtime. If, and only if, you start an online match, the App sends your chosen nickname, your chosen avatar and your in-game moves through Ably’s servers to your opponent and to any spectators of that match. This data is transient — it is used to run the match and is not stored by us afterwards. Please do not use your real name, email or any personal information as your nickname. See: https://ably.com/privacy

Analytics — GameAnalytics. The App uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It records gameplay events (matches started and finished, progression, in-game currency, ad views), together with your device model, operating system, application version, approximate country derived from your IP address, and a random identifier generated by the SDK and stored on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email or any account, and is not your Advertising ID. See: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

We use no other analytics or tracking SDK in this Android application.

3. Our own purposes and legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Advertising profiling and ad measurement are carried out by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies — we do not build advertising profiles and we do not personalise content. For our own processing we rely on the following purposes, in the wording of the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework:

  • Store and/or access information on a device — legal basis: consent. The analytics SDK's random identifier and your local game data are stored on your device.
  • Measure content performance — legal basis: consent. How far players get and which matches are finished, i.e. the gameplay events described in section 2.
  • Develop and improve services — legal basis: legitimate interest. We read those aggregate figures to balance and improve the game.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 4), and you may object to processing based on our legitimate interest by writing to the contact address above.

4. Advertising consent and personalisation

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used, and the same message covers the analytics purposes listed in section 3. You may decline; the App remains fully playable and will serve non-personalised ads instead. You can change your choice at any time from your device settings, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on Android.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

6. Deleting your data

Nine to Wonderland (Aiwa Int) has no accounts on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can remove everything yourself:

  • Local data (progress, settings, statistics): Android Settings → Apps → Nine to Wonderland → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app. This deletes all data listed in section 2 immediately and permanently.
  • Cloud save stored in your own Google account via Play Games: open the Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → choose to delete data for this game only. This removes the saved game; deletion is immediate and nothing is retained afterwards.
  • Advertising identifier: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

No data is retained after these steps. Questions: aiwainit2023@gmail.com

7. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Links

Ads may link to third-party sites we do not operate. Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
